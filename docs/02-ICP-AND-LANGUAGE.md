# ICP and Language

## Who she is

Primary buyer: a Saudi woman, roughly 23 to 45, in Riyadh first, then Jeddah, Dammam, Makkah, Madinah, and the rest of the Kingdom.

She lives in an apartment or a villa she cares about. Guests come. She notices the smell at the door, the ceiling at night, and the dust that returns the same day she cleans. She buys on her phone, usually from Snapchat or TikTok, and she will not pay 199 SAR to a page that looks like a random catalog.

She pays cash on delivery. She answers the phone if the page told her a call is coming. She abandons the order if the form asks for too much, if the price jumps, or if the page feels imported.

Secondary reader: her sister, her mother, or the woman who sends her the ad. Copy still speaks to one woman, with تاء التأنيث.

## What she is afraid of

- She pays online and the box never matches the video.
- The oud smells like burnt sugar or cheap oil.
- The projector is a child's toy, harsh and blue.
- The "air purifier" is a fan with a perfume pad and a medical claim.
- The courier arrives before anyone called, or calls from a hidden number and she does not pick up.
- The page is in English, or in a dialect that is not hers.

## What makes her trust

- Arabic from the first pixel. Her name in the form. A Saudi mobile, not an email.
- One brand, three products, repeated until they feel intentional.
- COD written in the header, the product page, the cart, and the form.
- A human sentence: we call to confirm the address, then we ship.
- Specific enemies, not "جودة عالية" and "خامة ممتازة".
- Photos of a Saudi evening room, not a white studio with a European sofa. Until real photos exist, the sample images follow [06-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md](06-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md).

## Voice

Saudi white dialect. Light, understood in Riyadh and Jeddah. Feminine address. Short sentences. Concrete nouns.

Use: بيتكِ، ريحة، جو، كتمة، غبرة، مجلس، سهرة، الحين، يطلع، هادي، دافي، ثقيل، باهت.

Avoid:

- Moroccan: ديال، زعما، بزاف، حيت، أش
- Heavy Egyptian: أوي، إزاي، عايزة
- Ad-spam: حصري، لفترة محدودة، الحقّي، مجانًا، خصم، وفرّي، ٪
- Corporate: حلول متكاملة، تجربة فريدة، جودة لا تضاهى
- Medical: يعالج، ينقّي الرئة، يقتل البكتيريا، آمن للأطفال as a health claim

Write the pain before the product name. Then one proof. Then the price as a calm number.

## Pattern

1. Enemy, in her words.
2. Ritual name.
3. What it does in one line.
4. What it refuses to do (no charcoal, no white blast, no perfume pretending to be a filter).
5. Price ladder, framed as completing the house.
6. COD and the confirmation call.

## Numbers

Use Arabic-Indic digits in marketing sentences (١٩٩ ر.س) and Western digits in form fields, order IDs, and the sheet. Prices always say ر.س. Currency code in code and pixels is `SAR`.
