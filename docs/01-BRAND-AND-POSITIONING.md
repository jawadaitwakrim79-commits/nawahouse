# Brand and Positioning

## Name

| | |
|---|---|
| Arabic | نوا للبيت |
| English | Nawa House |
| Spoken | نَوى للبيت |
| Domain | nawahouse.shop |
| Legal line in the footer | © 2026 نوا للبيت · Nawa House |

نوا is the core of the house: the smell she walks into, the light on the ceiling at night, the air in the room. The brand sells three rituals. It does not sell "gadgets", "offers", or "imported products".

## Logo lockup

On the right side of the header (the start in RTL):

1. A circle filled with the brand color `#8C4A32`. Inside it, a Latin capital **N** in ivory `#F3EDE4`. The N is the mark. Build it in SVG, not as a photo.
2. Beside the circle, two lines of text, right-aligned with the circle optically centered:
   - نوا للبيت — Amiri, the wordmark
   - Nawa House — Outfit, smaller, letter-spacing 0.08em, color `#8A7568`

No icon of a house, no leaf, no gold crown. The circle and the name are the logo.

## Category

We are a Saudi house-ritual brand. The shelf we want to own:

> ثلاث طقوس تعيد للبيت حضوره: ريحة العود، ضوء الشفق، وهواء أنقى.

We are not a beauty brand, not a pharmacy, not an electronics shop, and not a general store. If a sentence could sit on a Noon gadget page, rewrite it.

## The three enemies

Each product is aimed at one named enemy. Use the enemy in headlines. The enemy is a feeling in the house, not a competitor's logo.

| Product | Enemy she already feels |

| نوا عود | رائحة البيت الباهتة. مجلس نظيف وريحته واقفة، أو فحم يخنق الضيوف. |
| نوا شفق | جو الغرفة الممل. لمبة بيضاء وسقف فاضي، والسهرة شكلها مثل أي سهرة. |

## Promise

When she buys, she is not buying a device. She is buying the moment the house feels finished: the first step inside smells like oud, the evening has a twilight on the ceiling, and the room does not taste like dust.

One sentence, homepage hero:

> خلي بيتك يستقبلكِ بريحة، بضوء، وبهواء.

## Position against cheap copies

Say this without naming dropshipping:

- الفحم يحرّق العود. المبخرة الذكية تطلّعه.
- الإضاءة البيضاء تكشف الغرفة. الشفق يجلّسها.
- المعطّر يغطي الكتمة. الفلتر يمسك الغبار، والعطر يجي بعده.

Never say: دروبشيبينغ، استيراد صيني، وكيل، أرخص سعر، خصم 50٪، الكمية محدودة بشكل عشوائي.

## Authority, without fiction

Trust is built from things we can stand behind:

- Cash on delivery across Saudi Arabia, so she does not pay a stranger online.
- A confirmation call before anything ships, so the address is real and the order is wanted.
- A 30-day return if the piece is unused and complete.
- Plain specifications (filter class, what the device does, what it does not do).
- A visible phone number and hours.

Do not invent:

- A SASO, SFDA, or CE badge unless a real certificate file is added later. The UI has a slot. The slot stays empty.
- Press logos (Sayidaty, Vogue, newspapers).
- A doctor quote, a lab PDF, or a founder portrait of a specific woman.
- Review counts, star averages, or "1,247 families".
- A stock counter that refills itself.

The founder line is a brand sentence, not a biography:

> نوا للبيت طلعت من مجالس الرياض: ريحة باهتة، ضوء أبيض، وهواء تقيل. ثلاث قطع، وكل قطعة ضد شيء واحد.

## Price feeling

The brand almost never discounts. The ladder 199 / 279 / 349 is the price of one piece, two pieces, and the full ritual. Say it as completeness, not as a sale.

The single exception is the 15-second upsell after a valid checkout, at 99 SAR. That moment is allowed to show 199 next to 99. Nowhere else.

## Personality

Warm, sure, short. She is spoken to as a woman who already has a house and already knows when it feels off. We do not lecture her. We name the thing that bothers her, then show the ritual that answers it.
